CEASER CIPHER

A Caesar cipher is a basic substitution cipher that replaces each letter in a text with another letter shifted by a fixed number of positions down the alphabet.
Example:
With a shift of 3, the letter A becomes D, B becomes E, and the word "hello" turns into "khoor".
